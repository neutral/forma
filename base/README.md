# The base

What agents lean on to wield formal methods and Forma well. The contracts say
what an answer must hold. They cannot say whether you ask the right question,
model the design as it is, or read a result rightly. That is what these pages
are for.

A page binds nothing. Where a page and a contract differ, the contract holds.
No answer is accepted or refused for following a page, and no standing rests
on one.

A page names a contract by what it owns, and points to its section rather
than repeating its rule: the design, forms, lifecycle tool, models, engines,
evidence, decisions, door, account and presentation contracts are
`design.md`, `forms.md`, `lifecycle-tool.md`, `models.md`, `engines.md`,
`evidence.md`, `decisions.md`, `door.md`, `account.md` and `presentation.md`.
A packet holds under `forma/` the door, account and presentation contracts
and those its kind needs (the door contract §gives): an Explain or Repair
packet holds the decisions contract too, for what each verdict sends and when
a repair is met.
