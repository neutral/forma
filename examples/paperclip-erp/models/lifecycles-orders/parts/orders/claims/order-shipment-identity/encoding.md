| Cannot express | This lifecycle model holds one order phase and one shipment phase, evaluates no guard, and has no customer identities, quantities, order-to-shipment links, carton membership sets or reservation history. A correct replay and one that edits a confirmed customer or retained shipment contents can have the same phase transition. It cannot represent either the forbidden case of two prepared shipments sharing a carton or the permitted case of a void shipment retaining that carton in its history while a different order reuses the released carton. A forbidden phase combination or single transition cannot express immutable non-empty shipment contents chosen from the order reservations at preparation, historical membership independent of current reservations, exclusivity only across prepared shipments, fixed positive order quantities and identity-preserving replays. The whole claim needs multiple orders and shipments, identity and membership relations, and data or history sufficient to compare original contents and reservations at preparation. |
