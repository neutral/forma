  ## Recording a batch
  
  A finished batch enters once, with its identities and quantity checked, and starts on quality hold. Repeating that batch receipt does not create more cartons. QA releases the batch after its inspection. Only released cartons can be reserved. Available means released, on hand and unreserved. We do not correct counts, scrap batches, transfer stock or substitute products in this slice.
Cites design/brief/stock.md#holds-after-release:
  ## Holds after release
  
  QA can put a released batch back on hold, even after a carton is reserved, picked or collected. A hold immediately blocks new reservations and collection of the affected cartons still on hand. It does not erase existing reservations: those orders wait for release or the customer cancels the uncollected remainder. QA can release the batch again.
  
  Each QA decision has its own operation identity and names the batch's current quality revision. A successful hold or release increments that revision. A new operation with a stale revision is refused. Replaying a successful operation returns its recorded result without changing the current quality state, even if newer decisions have intervened. Releasing a batch after a new hold therefore requires a fresh QA decision; an old release message cannot lift the hold.
  
  For cartons collected before a later hold, staff can find every affected shipment and customer from the retained batch links. We examine the completeness of those links, not whether support contacts customers or retrieves goods. A later hold cannot make an earlier authorised collection unhappen.
Cites design/brief/orders.md#confirming-an-order:
  ## Confirming an order
  
  Confirmation succeeds only when enough released cartons are available and the customer has credit for the whole order. It reserves particular cartons and commits the full order value against that customer's credit in the same operation. If either check fails, neither stock nor credit changes. Two orders competing for the last cartons or the last credit cannot both take them. After a successful confirmation, confirming the same order again returns its recorded result and adds nothing. A refusal for stock or credit leaves the draft retryable when circumstances change.

  ## Picking and preparing a shipment
  
  A picker gathers scans, then submits one preparation command with a new shipment identity, its order and its complete non-empty set of cartons. Scan gathering before submission changes no stock records. Preparation checks all cartons are reserved for the order, released and not already in another prepared shipment, then marks them picked together. Shipment membership is fixed after that. An exact replay adds nothing; reusing its identity with different contents is refused, even after collection or cancellation. There may be more than one prepared shipment for an order. A shipment is prepared, collected or void; collected and void shipments cannot be edited. Packing and printing a label do not count as collection or create an invoice.
