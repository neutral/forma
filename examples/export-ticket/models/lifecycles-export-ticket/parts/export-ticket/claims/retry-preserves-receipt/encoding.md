| Cannot express | The ticket-state lifecycle records expiry and claimed status, but not which request key owns a durable receipt, its original identity, receipt creations, additional claims or the identity in a delivered reply. Thus it cannot test the claim's antecedent for the retried key. Both retry-claim and return-retry-receipt are self-loops in active-claimed and expired-claimed. Preserving and returning the original receipt and creating or returning a different receipt can have the same phase projection. Forbidding a self-loop would also reject a retry that preserves and returns the original; one transition cannot cover both expiry phases and both handler and reply effects. The whole claim needs key-indexed original receipts, creation and claim history, and delivered reply values. Neither a forbidden phase combination nor one transition expresses those requirements on this model. |
