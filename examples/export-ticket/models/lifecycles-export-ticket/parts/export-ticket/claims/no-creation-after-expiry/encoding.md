| Cannot express | The ticket-state lifecycle records expiry and claimed status, but not receipt creations or request-key history. Both complete and retry-claim create fresh receipts on expired-unclaimed to expired-claimed and on expired-claimed to expired-claimed. These are four distinct transitions, whereas a lifecycle encoding can forbid only one transition, with one source, action and destination. Any one omits other post-expiry creations. Forbidding the expired-claimed phase instead also rejects a receipt created before expiry and correctly preserved by expiry. The whole claim needs every receipt creation to be checked against prior expiry, across original completions, retries and overlapping keys; no permitted single transition or phase combination expresses it on this model. |
