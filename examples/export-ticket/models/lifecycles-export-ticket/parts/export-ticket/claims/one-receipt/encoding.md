| Cannot express | The ticket-state lifecycle records only expiry and claimed status, not receipt identities, creation counts, request keys or completion history. A claimed phase does not distinguish one receipt from several. Both complete and retry-claim can create another receipt on active-claimed to active-claimed or expired-claimed to expired-claimed; one forbidden transition cannot cover these different actions and source phases. Forbidding a claimed phase would reject the allowed first receipt, and returning or fetching that same receipt must remain allowed. The whole claim needs distinct receipt identities or creation history across original requests, overlapping keys and retries, which this phase-only model and its single-transition or phase-combination encodings do not provide. |
