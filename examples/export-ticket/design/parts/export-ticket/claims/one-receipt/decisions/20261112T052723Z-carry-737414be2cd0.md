Cites design/brief/overview.md#purpose:
  ## Purpose
  
  A small service lets the recipient of a prepared export claim a durable receipt. The receipt unlocks that export. A ticket is intended for one successful claim, not one network packet. Fetching an already-created receipt is a read; it does not create another receipt. Examine the design, not an implementation.
Cites design/brief/overview.md#required-behavior:
  ## Required behavior
  
  No new receipt may be created after the ticket expires. At most one receipt may ever be created for a ticket, even when two request keys overlap. A claim completed before expiry must remain possible; refusing every claim is not an acceptable design. Expiry and concurrent request keys must remain possible.
  
  Whether a recipient may fetch an already-created receipt after expiry is not yet decided. Put that question to the developer; do not treat creating a new receipt and reading the original receipt as the same action.
Cites design/brief/overview.md#scale:
  ## Scale
  
  1. Do not create access after expiry. 2. Do not claim the same ticket more than once. 3. Preserve an ordinary successful claim and distinguish receipt retrieval.
Cites design/brief/changes/v2.md#change-retry-a-claim-after-an-uncertain-reply:
  # Change: retry a claim after an uncertain reply
  
  This changes the claim design after the first examination and its repair. Keep that repair unless the statements here explicitly replace it. The recipient may retry the same request key after receiving no reply, including after expiry. There may be one retry per key in this finite examination.
  
  The proposed retry handler remembers which keys passed Check. If a retry has a remembered passing check, it executes Complete again, even if an earlier Complete for that key has stored a receipt. It creates a fresh receipt on each execution and returns that new receipt. Reply delivery may be lost again. This handler bypasses the normal first-completion gate; it is the new design to examine, not an instruction to regard that behavior as acceptable.
  
  The required result of retry is the original durable receipt for that key, including after expiry. A retry must create no additional receipt and confer no additional claim. A key that never completed must not gain a new receipt after expiry. The previous one-receipt-per-ticket and no-creation-after-expiry requirements still apply. Preserve successful completion before expiry and retrieval of an existing receipt. The developer has selected retrieval after expiry as permitted; retain the actual decision as its authority.
  
  Keep one ticket and two request keys. Include the first completion and at most one retry per key, no more than three attempted receipt creations, and one expiry event. State the added limit and any previous findings whose basis changed. Lost replies are observations, not rollback of store actions.
