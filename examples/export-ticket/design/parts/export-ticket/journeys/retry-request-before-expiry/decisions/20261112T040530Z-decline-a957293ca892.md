## Words

```text
The required result of a retry is the original durable receipt for that key, including after expiry (design/brief/changes/v2.md#change-retry-a-claim-after-an-uncertain-reply). This journey stops once the retry is sent while the ticket is active; it should go on to the service answering that retry by returning the same stored receipt r-a to the recipient, with no new receipt and no new claim. Stopping before the result would let a design that never answers a retry pass.
```

  # Change: retry a claim after an uncertain reply
  
  This changes the claim design after the first examination and its repair. Keep that repair unless the statements here explicitly replace it. The recipient may retry the same request key after receiving no reply, including after expiry. There may be one retry per key in this finite examination.
  
  The proposed retry handler remembers which keys passed Check. If a retry has a remembered passing check, it executes Complete again, even if an earlier Complete for that key has stored a receipt. It creates a fresh receipt on each execution and returns that new receipt. Reply delivery may be lost again. This handler bypasses the normal first-completion gate; it is the new design to examine, not an instruction to regard that behavior as acceptable.
  
  The required result of retry is the original durable receipt for that key, including after expiry. A retry must create no additional receipt and confer no additional claim. A key that never completed must not gain a new receipt after expiry. The previous one-receipt-per-ticket and no-creation-after-expiry requirements still apply. Preserve successful completion before expiry and retrieval of an existing receipt. The developer has selected retrieval after expiry as permitted; retain the actual decision as its authority.
