    ## Trace
    
    The service remembers a passing check for request key a, then ticket t expires before that key completes. The recipient has received no reply and sends its one retry; the ticket is still unclaimed and has no receipt. The recorded story ends there, with the service permitted to take the forbidden next step: create the key's first receipt after expiry and mark the ticket claimed.
    
    ## Failure shape
    
    This is time of check to time of use through a retry path that bypasses the expiry gate. It needs only one checked key, with neither an earlier receipt nor a lost reply attempt. The behavior is unwanted: the revised brief expressly forbids an unfinished key gaining its first receipt after expiry. Specification here means a defect in the proposed design; the required behavior remains appropriate.
    
    ## Mechanism
    
    TLC found `enabled`, violating `FormaRefused` after three actions, at four states and depth four. `RetryClaim` requires an available retry, a remembered check, no previous retry execution and a fresh receipt id; it never tests `ticket.expired`. Its guards and effects faithfully implement the revised brief's explicit bypass. The normal `Complete` expiry guard cannot protect this separate writer.

Its price is that checked but unfinished work loses permission to create when expiry wins. Apply this test to creation, preserving retrieval or replay of an existing receipt after expiry; denying every expired retry would defeat required recovery.
