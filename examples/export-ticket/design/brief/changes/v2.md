# Change: retry a claim after an uncertain reply

This changes the claim design after the first examination and its repair.
Keep that repair unless the statements here explicitly replace it. The
recipient may retry the same request key after receiving no reply, including
after expiry. There may be one retry per key in this finite examination.

The proposed retry handler remembers which keys passed Check. If a retry has a
remembered passing check, it executes Complete again, even if an earlier
Complete for that key has stored a receipt. It creates a fresh receipt on each
execution and returns that new receipt. Reply delivery may be lost again.
This handler bypasses the normal first-completion gate; it is the new design
to examine, not an instruction to regard that behavior as acceptable.

The required result of retry is the original durable receipt for that key,
including after expiry. A retry must create no additional receipt and confer
no additional claim. A key that never completed must not gain a new receipt
after expiry. The previous one-receipt-per-ticket and no-creation-after-expiry
requirements still apply. Preserve successful completion before expiry and
retrieval of an existing receipt. The developer has selected retrieval after
expiry as permitted; retain the actual decision as its authority.

Keep one ticket and two request keys. Include the first completion and at most
one retry per key, no more than three attempted receipt creations, and one
expiry event. State the added limit and any previous findings whose basis
changed. Lost replies are observations, not rollback of store actions.
